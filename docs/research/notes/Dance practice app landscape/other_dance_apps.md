# Dance practice and learning apps outside salsa/bachata (other partner dances and dance generally)

Scope note: research done 2026-11-02 by web search (about 22 tool calls). App Store pages often show version dates without a year; where that happens it is said. Several "general" move-journal apps found here (Flow State, Dance Journal) use salsa/bachata as their examples, so they overlap with the salsa/bachata research too. They are listed because they are not tied to one dance.

Feature key used below (the user's app): F1 Today scheduling by frequency/urgency from logged sets; F2 personal figure library with variations, own recordings, start count and length in counts; F3 figure graph of positions with a gap report; F4 routines checked for timing and connection; F5 song library with ML beat tracking and the user tapping the 1; F6 player: real song + count in the user's own voice + clave + figure names called in time; F7 lesson videos with notes and sections looped/mirrored/slowed and linked to moves; F8 saved YouTube videos with bookmarked sections; F9 multi-user + Android app.

## 1. Learning platforms (Steezy, AI coaches, ballroom, WCS, tango, Lindy hop, K-pop/TikTok)

### Takeaway
The big learning platforms are course libraries: teacher videos with speed, loop and mirror controls. They overlap with F7 only, and only for their own content, not a person's own lesson videos. Partner-dance platforms (WCS, tango, ballroom) are video libraries or syllabus references. The one strong partner-dance precedent for a move graph is ballroom's "precedes/follows" tradition (Ballroom Genie, Dance Central routine builders). A new wave of 2025–2026 indie iOS apps (FYP Dance, LeapNow, STEPIN, Audition ME) does AI pose scoring for K-pop and TikTok covers. None of them schedules practice, keeps a personal figure graph, or counts over a real song in the user's voice.

- Ballroom Quest (GitHub, 0 stars, 15 commits): a 3D browser practice game. `figures.json` holds 151 figures across 22 dances, with steps as `[foot, direction, beats, turn°, count]` and neighbour rules `only_after`/`only_before`. It also has a drill builder from lesson notes, a weekly practice planner from practice history, a choreographer that maps figures to song sections, in-browser beat/bar analysis of your own song, and optional MediaPipe grading of your video. This is the closest single match to F3/F4 (plus a little of F1/F5) found anywhere, but it is a hobby repo with no users. — [GitHub](https://github.com/20Sam/ballroom-quest)

### Inferences
- Commercial learning platforms compete on content (teacher videos), not on organising a person's own material. Their overlap with the user's app is limited to F7-style playback controls on their own catalogue.
- Ballroom is the only partner-dance world where the "what can follow what" idea is already formalised and sold (precedes/follows tables, routine builders). Even there it is a fixed syllabus graph, not a personal one with a gap report.
- In 2025–2026 most new building is AI pose-scoring for solo choreography (K-pop/TikTok). That is a different axis from the user's app, which has no pose analysis.

- CLI Studios, iDance, Kinetix, Just Dance, "Tango Mentor", "WCS Pattern"/"Swing Pattern Library": not searched individually or not found. No user numbers or status for CLI Studios or Just Dance.

- Ratings for Dance Vision Syllabus and DanzDB were not retrieved.

## 2. Choreography and notation tools, and move diaries — does any model a move graph or transitions?

### Takeaway
Formation and staging tools (Stage Write, Choreographic, StageKeep) handle spacing on a stage, not partner figures. Move-diary apps are a growing 2025–2026 category: Flow State, Dance Journal, Sweet Dance Moves, DanceLib. One of them, **Flow State**, has a "Move Map" of what leads into and out of each move, built from combos you label, plus a community. It is the closest product to F2+F3, but it is a usage map, not positions with a gap report, and it has no counts or music. **Rhythm Review** (GitHub) has moves with count durations, patterns and SM-2 spaced repetition. That is the closest to F1+F2, but it is a 0-star hobby project.

### Cited Findings
- Stage Write (iPad, Jeff Whiting): documents staging, choreography and spacing for theatre, "aligned with the counts in the music". — [jeffwhiting.com](https://www.jeffwhiting.com/stage-write); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/hu/app/stage-write/id1004288568?l=hu)
- Choreographic: Dance Formation (iPad): formations on stage, 3D animated transitions, music sync, real-time collaboration. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/sg/app/choreographic-dance-formation/id1608391996)
- StageKeep: formation management app for creating and sharing formations with dancers. — [MaRS](https://app.marsdd.com/companies/stagekeep)
- "ArabesK": no results found.
- **Flow State: Dance Library** (iPhone, developer Kevin Kadak, v1.3.0, free + Pro at $69.99/yr or $6.99/mo; too few ratings to show):
  - Sessions log each dance day (styles, role, feeling, place, videos, notes) and count steps live.
  - "Combos" turn parts of videos into labelled move sequences.
  - The "Move Map" shows what leads into and out of each move and every combo using it.
  - Notes can be pinned to a moment in a video.
  - Community feed and Discover let you adopt others' combos.
  - Offline.
  - No music or count features described; bachata is used as its example.

  — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flow-state-dance-library/id6760927859)
- **Dance Journal** (iPhone and Android, free):
  - Save Instagram reels or class videos.
  - A move library by style and level.
  - Loop between set points at 0.25–1× with a fine-tune dial, frame by frame.
  - Record yourself beside the tutorial.
  - Notes pinned to the timeline.
  - Status "Working on it"/"Got it", with "Last practiced"/"Next move" indicators and monthly practice counts.
  - No scheduling-by-frequency, music or counts described.

  — [dancejournal.app](https://dancejournal.app/)
- **Sweet Dance Moves Library** (iOS, Buildandteach Media; v3.5; $2.99 lifetime or $9.99/yr; 5.0 from 2 ratings): save moves from Camera Roll, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok or Facebook; styles, sources and tags; practice lists for shows and competitions; slow motion; iCloud sync; no reminders or spaced repetition. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sweet-dance-moves/id6749212354)
- DanceLib (iOS and Android): video library with community features and a personal uploads section; favourite moves; progress over time. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dancelib/id6670781631); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.companyname.dancelib.maui&hl=en-US)
- **Rhythm Review** (GitHub, 0 stars, 10 commits; Svelte + Vite + TypeScript + Drizzle + Postgres, on Vercel): moves with names, descriptions and count durations; patterns as sequences; practice sets; SM-2 spaced-repetition scheduling; practice logs. No music or video. — [GitHub](https://github.com/Waidhoferj/rhythm-review)
- Ballroom Quest (see section 1) is the only tool found that validates a chained routine (`check_routine`) and suggests bridging figures for broken links. — [GitHub](https://github.com/20Sam/ballroom-quest)
- Ballroom Genie offers suggested precedes and follows over a fixed catalogue. — [ballroomgenie.com](https://www.ballroomgenie.com/)

### Inferences
- The idea of a "personal move library" is not new: at least four store apps launched in 2024–2026. A transitions map built from a personal library also exists (Flow State's Move Map).
- What remains distinct is the combination of:
  - positions as the nodes, with a computed gap report of missing transitions (Flow State only records what you did; it does not tell you what is missing);
  - start counts and lengths in counts on each figure;
  - routines checked for both timing and connection against a personal graph. Only the hobby Ballroom Quest does connection checks, against a fixed syllabus.
- Rhythm Review's stack (Svelte + Drizzle) and features (counts, patterns, SM-2) are strikingly close to the user's app, but it has no music, video or graph, and no users.

### Gaps
- No dance-notation software (Labanotation editors such as LabanWriter, or Benesh) was researched in this pass.
- No user counts for Flow State, Dance Journal or DanceLib; launch dates lack years on store pages.

## 3. Video practice tools (loop, mirror, slow, bookmark sections) — any linking sections to a personal move library?

### Takeaway
Loop/mirror/slow is commodity. Many 2025–2026 iOS apps do it, several with 8-count section splitting and recording yourself beside the teacher. Classic coaching video apps died: Coach's Eye was retired in 2022, and Hudl Technique was handed off to OnForm in 2021. Linking a video moment to a move exists in Flow State (combos labelled inside videos, notes that mention moves) and loosely in Dance Journal (a saved clip is a move). No tool was found that links a looping section of a lesson video or a saved YouTube video to a specific variation in a figure graph, and blocks deleting the video while it is linked (F7/F8 as built).

### Cited Findings
- **LeapNow** (iOS): 8-count sections, mirror, slow, A-B loop, teacher-vs-you pose and timing comparison. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/cd/app/id6771540435)
- **Dance.io｜Mirror & Loop & Slow** (iOS, developer Yuhan Liu, v1.3.7; €6.99/mo, €24.99 half-yearly, €59.99 lifetime; too few ratings):
  - A-B loop, speed, mirror.
  - Camera overlay.
  - "Visual and audio beat overlay for counting".
  - Side-by-side with your recording.
  - "Move-by-move video notes".

  — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/dance-io-mirror-loop-slow/id6748628387?l=en-GB)
- **DanceBeat** (iOS, Jianyue Wang, v1.0.4): automatic beat detection that splits the music into measures, 8-count navigation, loop, speed, mirror, original-vs-practice comparison, frame-by-frame scrubbing; videos stay on the device. No voice counting mentioned. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/co/app/dancebeat/id6757979636)
- FYP Dance: loop, slow motion, side by side, pose match score. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759196501)
- Loreo – Learn Choreo Fast: local-first choreo learning tool launched on Product Hunt; current availability unclear. — [hunted.space](https://hunted.space/product/loreo-learn-choreo-fast)
- Loop Loop – Practice Perfect (iOS) and SymPlayer – Master Movement (iOS mirror player) are further generic loop/mirror players. — [Loop Loop](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/loop-loop-practice-perfect/id6740039956?l=ar); [SymPlayer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1048785434)
- **Coach's Eye** shut down; the date is 31 March 2022 per vendor pages, September 2022 per AlternativeTo. Cloud footage was deleted at shutdown. — [V1 Sports](https://swing-analysis.v1sports.com/swing-analysis/coachs-eye-alternatives); [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/coach-s-eye/about)
- **Hudl Technique** (formerly Ubersense) was discontinued; its partners took it over as OnForm (2021). — [glennpaulley.ca](https://glennpaulley.ca/curling/2021/08/24/moving-to-onform-from-hudl-technique/); [Hudl support](https://twww.hudl.com/support/technique)
- Flow State turns video parts into labelled combos and pins feedback to moments that can mention moves. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flow-state-dance-library/id6760927859)
- Dance Journal saves Instagram reels or class videos as moves, loops a section between set points, and pins notes to the timeline. — [dancejournal.app](https://dancejournal.app/)
- Sweet Dance Moves saves moves from YouTube, Instagram and TikTok links (with thumbnails); speed controls only for local videos. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sweet-dance-moves/id6749212354)

### Inferences
- F7's playback controls (loop, mirror, 0.25–1×) are table stakes in 2026. The user's differentiators are the structure (saved spots/sections per video, linked to a variation or drill, shown as a looping card on the move's page) and downloading YouTube to keep it (F8). Competitors either store a link, import a file, or rely on share-sheet imports.
- Beat-aware video navigation (DanceBeat, Dance.io, LeapNow's 8-count sections) is the closest outside equivalent to the user's beat grid. It is applied to choreography videos, though, not to songs used to rehearse a partner routine.

### Gaps
- Status of "Dancify" and of the specific name "Learn Choreo" not confirmed. Browser extensions (Video Speed Controller, YouTube loopers) were not researched in this pass.

## 4. Beat and count tools (counting 8s, finding the 1, voice counting over music)

### Takeaway
Beat detection plus 8-count splitting exists in solo-choreo video apps (DanceBeat, Dance.io's beat overlay, LeapNow) and in the hobby Ballroom Quest (in-browser beat/bar analysis of your own song). WCS has a metronome rhythm trainer. No app found plays a real song with a voice counting 1–8 aligned to the song's beats, let alone in the user's own recorded voice, or calls figure names in time.

### Cited Findings
- DanceBeat: automatic beat detection, measure split, 8-count navigation (video, not voice). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/co/app/dancebeat/id6757979636)
- Dance.io: "visual and audio beat overlay for counting". — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/dance-io-mirror-loop-slow/id6748628387?l=en-GB)
- Westiefied's WCS Rhythm Training App has a built-in metronome. — [westiefied.com](https://westiefied.com/category/training/)
- Rhythm Count (iPhone): speaks numbers at custom intervals; a counting tool, not music-aligned as far as I could confirm. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6753152739)
- DanceTime Deluxe: an older educational metronome with an instructor's voice counting "whether or not the music is playing"; current availability unconfirmed. — [Lesson Planet](https://lessonplanet.com/teachers/dancetime-deluxe)
- An Upwork job post asks for a rhythm-coaching app with "voice callouts over uploaded music" and speed/looping. This shows demand, but it is not an existing product. — [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Develop-mobile-app-for-rhythm-coaching_~022076818167602623023)
- Ballroom Quest: tempo presets, metronome, backings with dance-specific accents, in-browser beat/bar/section analysis of the player's own song; a "choreographer" assigns figures to song sections. — [GitHub](https://github.com/20Sam/ballroom-quest)
- A Cambridge student group project, "Dance Practice Assistant", exists (details not fetched). — [cst.cam.ac.uk](https://group-projects.cst.cam.ac.uk/Dance_Practice_Assistant.html)

### Inferences
- "Find the 1" by user taps over an ML beat grid, with the count then spoken in the user's own voice over the real track, appears to have no shipped equivalent outside salsa/bachata. The Upwork post suggests people want it.

### Gaps
- Named apps "Count the Beat", "5678 app" and "Countdown" were not found by these searches. A store-level search for "dance counter" may find small utilities.
- The contents of the Cambridge "Dance Practice Assistant" project were not checked.

## 5. Which of the user's features have NO equivalent (outside salsa/bachata)?

### Takeaway
Every single feature has a partial cousin somewhere. The integration has none: a personal figure graph with positions and a gap report, routines checked for timing and connection, a beat-gridded song library, and a player that calls the routine's figures over the real song in the user's own counting voice, all tied to lesson-video sections. The nearest wholes are two 0-star GitHub hobby projects (Ballroom Quest; Rhythm Review) and one young commercial app (Flow State).

### Cited Findings (feature by feature, closest outside match)
- **F1 Today scheduling by frequency/urgency.** Rhythm Review uses SM-2 spaced repetition ([GitHub](https://github.com/Waidhoferj/rhythm-review)). Ballroom Quest's weekly practice planner works from practice history ([GitHub](https://github.com/20Sam/ballroom-quest)). Dance Journal shows "Last practiced"/"Next move" ([dancejournal.app](https://dancejournal.app/)). DANCELAB has reminders and habits ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ethanmbruno.dancelabai&hl=en_US)). No commercial app found schedules drills by frequency-derived urgency.
- **F2 personal figure library with variations, own videos, start count and length.** Flow State, Dance Journal, Sweet Dance Moves and DanceLib cover the library and videos. Only Rhythm Review stores count durations; DanzDB stores step counts for a fixed catalogue ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759789833)). Variations of a figure were not seen anywhere.
- **F3 figure graph with a gap report.** Flow State's Move Map shows leads-into/out-of from your own combos ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flow-state-dance-library/id6760927859)). Ballroom Genie and technique books give fixed precedes/follows ([ballroomgenie.com](https://www.ballroomgenie.com/)). Ballroom Quest's `suggest_bridges` uses a fixed syllabus ([GitHub](https://github.com/20Sam/ballroom-quest)). **No personal gap report found.**
- **F4 routines checked for timing and connection.** Ballroom Quest `check_routine` (hobby, fixed syllabus); Dance Central Waltz/Foxtrot routine builders ([dance-forums.com](https://www.dance-forums.com/threads/resources-on-designing-ballroom-choreography.53542/)). Stage Write documents choreography to counts but does not check it ([jeffwhiting.com](https://www.jeffwhiting.com/stage-write)).
- **F5 song library with ML beats and the user tapping the 1.** DanceBeat's beat detection and measure split works on choreo videos ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/co/app/dancebeat/id6757979636)). Ballroom Quest analyses a song in the browser. **No user-anchored "1" found.**
- **F6 voice count in the user's own voice + clave + figure calls in time over a real song.** Only metronomes (Westiefied), interval speakers (Rhythm Count) and an Upwork wish. **No equivalent found.**
- **F7 lesson videos with notes, sections looped/mirrored/slowed and linked to moves.** Playback controls are everywhere (Steezy, LeapNow, Dance.io, Dance Journal). Linking a moment to a move exists in Flow State. **Section-to-variation links that block deletion: none found.**
- **F8 saved YouTube videos with bookmarked sections.** Sweet Dance Moves saves YouTube/Instagram/TikTok links; Dance Journal imports Instagram reels. Downloading YouTube server-side to keep it was not seen. (It may also be a licensing reason commercial apps avoid it; that is an inference.)
- **F9 multi-user + Android.** Common: Dance Journal and DanceLib are on Android. Many of the new competitors (Flow State, Dance.io, DanceBeat, LeapNow, FYP Dance, Sweet Dance Moves) are iOS-only per their listings.

### Inferences
- The user's idea is **not new as a category**. "Personal dance move library" apps are proliferating in 2025–2026, and one already maps transitions. It **is new as a combination**, and in three specific places: (a) a computed gap report over a personal position graph; (b) routines validated for timing and connection against that personal graph; (c) the music-aligned player with the user's own counting voice and figure calls.
- Two independent hobby repos (Ballroom Quest, Rhythm Review) reached parts of the same design, both dated recently. That suggests the idea is "in the air" among developer-dancers, but nobody has shipped it commercially.
- Partner-dance structure (positions, leads into/out of) is best served today in ballroom, through fixed syllabi. A personal, user-defined graph for WCS, Lindy hop, tango, zouk or kizomba was not found at all.

### Gaps
- No search covered zouk- or kizomba-specific apps; none surfaced incidentally.
- Activity and user numbers for most indie apps are unknown: App Store pages hide the year and show "not enough ratings".
- Possible apps missed because store search was not done directly, with web search only.
