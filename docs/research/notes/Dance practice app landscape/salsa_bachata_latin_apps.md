# Salsa, bachata and Latin partner-dance apps: what exists (as of 2026-11-02)

Scope: apps, web tools and projects made for salsa, bachata, rueda, kizomba and zouk dancers, compared with the user's app. Its features are: (1) a Today screen that schedules drills by urgency; (2) a figure library with variations, own recordings, start count and length; (3) a figure graph with positions and a gap report; (4) routines checked for timing and connection; (5) a song library with ML beat analysis and a tapped 1; (6) a player with a 1–8 voice count in the user's own voice, clave and figure-name calls over a real song; (7) lessons with looped, mirrored and slowed video sections linked to figures; (8) saved YouTube videos with bookmarked sections; (9) multi-user with an Android app.

Method note: about 30 searches and page fetches. App Store pages often hide the year on version dates. Search summaries sometimes reported "nothing found" for apps that do exist (Flow State, for example). Treat download and rating figures as snapshots.

## Which salsa/bachata/Latin apps exist, what do they do, and are they alive?

### Takeaway
The space has three groups. **Rhythm and count trainers** are mature and the most popular: Salsa Rhythm has about 3.4K ratings and Dancetime about 100. **Online course platforms** are video catalogues of someone else's moves. A wave of **personal move-journal apps**, mostly iOS-only, launched in 2025–2026, most with almost no ratings yet. None of the brand names in the brief turned up as a standalone app. "Salsa Beat Machine" is marketing wording used by Salsa Rhythm.

### Cited Findings
**Rhythm and count trainers (generated rhythm, not your songs)**
- **Salsa Rhythm** (iOS by District One Mobile; the Android listing is under CodeMagic LTD). It is a salsa band you build up instrument by instrument: clave, piano, congas, timbales, bongos, bass, maracas, güiro, cowbell, plus an instructor voice counting On1 or On2, with 2/3 or 3/2 clave. It has 4.7★ from about 3.4K ratings and costs $2.99 on iOS. It plays its own loops and does not analyse outside music. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/salsa-rhythm/id379868541); [AppFollow tracker](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/salsa-rhythm/379868541?country=us); [search summary of listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id379868541)
- **BeatLab** (Ideatic) has separate apps for Salsa Rhythms, Bachata Rhythms, merengue and reggaeton. They use recorded real instruments that you can mute or solo, a counting voice to "find the 1", adjustable BPM, lessons on clave, majao and dembow, and they work offline. iOS and Android. The Salsa Rhythms iOS listing has too few ratings to show (one tracker shows 5.0 from 2). — [beatlab.app](https://beatlab.app/en/); [Salsa Rhythms – BeatLab](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6749232577); [Bachata Rhythms – BeatLab](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6748954690)
- **Salsa Souls Rhythm Machine** is a web rhythm machine with a spoken 1–8 count or a 1-2-3 / 5-6-7 count. It generates the rhythm itself. — [salsasouls.co.uk](https://www.salsasouls.co.uk/salsa-timing/)
- Other names that appeared without details checked: Tap Tap Salsa, Clave Master, SalsaStep Pro (free beta), Dance Kitchen (salsa and bachata footwork, $7.99, adjustable BPM). — [Tap Tap Salsa](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id495599034); [SalsaStep Pro](https://apps.apple.com/ee/app/salsastep-pro/id6480377680); [Dance Kitchen](https://apps.apple.com/ms/app/dance-kitchen/id6443764461)

**Count over real music: listening, analysis and musicality**
- **Dancetime – Salsa Beat Finder** (Dancetime Technologies LLC; iOS and Android). It listens to music from any app or speaker, recognises the song by audio fingerprint, then counts 1–8 with On1/On2 step animations and a voice count you can switch on or off. You can mute any of the 8 beats, or cut beats in and out on an "Interval" loop so you keep the count yourself. Vibrate pulses on beat 1 or on 1 and 5. A game mode checks your taps on 1 and 5. A social-video feed is counted along to the beat. The music library holds about 15–20K salsa songs (the site's own numbers disagree) in 5 difficulty levels, linked to Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube. Bachata is in beta. Freemium: Premium unlocks unlimited detection, with in-app purchases from $2.39 to $24.99. iOS shows 4.7★ from 102 ratings. v1.0 is dated 01/31/2024 and v5.6 Oct 22 (2026 per the © year), so it is actively developed. — [dancetimeapp.com](https://dancetimeapp.com/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dancetime-salsa-beat-finder/id6476049765); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dancetime.dancetimeandroid&hl=en_US)
- **Show Me the Counts (SMTC)** works with any style and your own music library. It marks named, colour-coded sections of a song with notes and tags, loops sections, sets speed from 50% to 150%, and offers AI stem separation (Pro). Pro also adds section sharing and plays sections across tracks by tag. iOS and Android; free core with a 7-day Pro trial. No automatic count is described. — [showmethecounts.com](https://showmethecounts.com/)
- **Cadencia** (Leonid Shevtsov, Ukraine; iOS and Android; early access/alpha). It makes song maps for salsa and bachata: you import your own tracks, **set the tempo and beat 1 yourself**, mark sections and accents by hand, and a live count follows. It loops and slows sections, needs no account, and keeps data on the device. "Coming later: move vocabulary, party mode, choreography builder." — [cadencia.dance](https://cadencia.dance/)
- **Dentro** (bachata) splits any bachata track into vocals, guitars, bass and percussion, maps the 8-count with a highlighted beat, and syncs lyrics to a translation. Free tier, or Pro at $8/month for 15 separations and 15 count-mapped songs. — [getdentro.com](https://getdentro.com/)
- **MUZO: Bachata Musicality** (Android, possibly more) teaches finding the 1 with real songs. Each instrument gets its own track, there are tap tests, mini-games and a daily challenge. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.muzo.lessons&hl=en_US)
- **bachata-music.com** is a web trainer with a voiced beat count over 5,000+ bachata tracks, instrument stems and an interactive metronome. It comes from a search snippet; the site would not load, so this is unverified. — [search result](https://bachata-music.com/)
- **BachataOn1** is a free tool for finding the 1 using YouTube social-dance videos that experienced dancers have tagged. — [bachataon1.com](https://bachataon1.com/en/musicality)

**Learning content platforms (someone else's moves)**
- **TheDanceDojo** has 300+ salsa lessons (On1 and On2) and a bundle with 100+ bachata lessons. Reviewers mention slowing down and rewatching. — [thedancedojo.com](https://thedancedojo.com/product/salsa/)
- **Salsa-bit** offers scene-by-scene video lessons you can loop, slow and cast to a TV. **The New Salsa Academy** (Meta Quest VR) offers mirror practice in passthrough; one reviewer says tracking is unreliable. — [salsa-bit.com](https://www.salsa-bit.com/salsa-lessons); [Meta store](https://www.meta.com/experiences/the-new-salsa-academy/7386321584720513/)
- Others: Bachata Dance Academy (iOS), Bachatapp Studio (technique and music), DancePath (kizomba and zouk lesson catalogue), Kizomba To Go. — [Bachata Dance Academy](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6475698623); [Bachatapp Studio](https://apps.apple.com/la/app/bachatapp-studio/id6745797397); [DancePath](https://apps.apple.com/se/app/dancepath/id6757916482)
- **Salsa Practice** (web app by forum user AlexisSalsa, posted 2024-01-01) has a Voice Coach that gives the beats and walks you through solo or couple combinations by level (only Absolute Beginner and Beginner 1), plus a beat-by-beat footwork mode. No replies are visible on the thread. — [salsaforums thread](https://www.salsaforums.com/threads/i-made-an-app-to-practice-salsa-steps-at-home.42516/); [salsapractice.web.app](https://salsapractice.web.app/)

**Personal move journals and libraries (new in 2025–2026, mostly iOS)**: details are in the third question below.

### Inferences
- Only the rhythm trainers have meaningful user counts (Salsa Rhythm about 3.4K ratings, Dancetime about 100). Almost every personal-library app shows "not enough ratings", so this niche is young and unproven.
- Bachata tooling is growing fast around stems and song analysis (Dentro, MUZO, BeatLab, Dancetime's beta).

### Gaps
- No standalone apps were found for Salsa Underground, Dance Flavours, Demetrio & Nicole, DanceFlix or a Bachata Sensual platform. They seem to be web course sites, and their app status could not be confirmed.
- Google Play install counts for Dancetime, Salsa Rhythm and MUZO were not retrieved (the Play page fetch came back empty).
- Prices are missing for Dancetime Premium per tier, SMTC Pro and Cadencia.

## Do any apps count your music for you, find the 1, or call moves in time with the music?

### Takeaway
Yes for counting: Dancetime does it live over real songs with an automatic voice count, and Dentro and bachata-music.com count over a catalogue or your uploads. Cadencia uses a hand-tapped 1, like the user's anchor. **No app found combines a real song, an automatic beat grid, a user-anchored 1, a count in your own recorded voice, clave, and figure calls timed to the music.** Move calling exists only over a metronome or BPM (Ritmo, Rueda Simulator, Salsa Practice), not over a beat-analysed song.

### Cited Findings
- Dancetime: song recognition, beat lock and a spoken 1–8 count over music playing from any source. Counting depends on a 15–20K song catalogue (fingerprinting), not on analysing arbitrary files. — [dancetimeapp.com](https://dancetimeapp.com/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dancetime-salsa-beat-finder/id6476049765)
- Cadencia: you "set the tempo and beat 1 so the count lines up" on your own imported tracks, and a live count follows the structure you marked. It does not detect structure. — [cadencia.dance](https://cadencia.dance/)
- Dentro maps the 8-count on bachata tracks as a paid service ($8/month for 15 songs). — [getdentro.com](https://getdentro.com/)
- **Ritmo: Dance Practice** (Coconut Donkey LLC, iOS, first released 2026-06-18, v1.1, 0 ratings, fewer than 1K downloads). It "calls out each move in a sequence" so you can practise eyes-up. You build routines from a built-in salsa and bachata move library and set the tempo per routine (the slider shows 160). The listing does not mention playing a real song. — [mwm.ai listing summary](https://mwm.ai/apps/ritmo-dance-practice/6778009666)
- **Rueda de Casino Call Simulator** (web MVP at ruedasimulator.com; GitHub RNPGhost/RuedaSimulator). Calls go into a queue and "wait for their place in the measure" at a set BPM. A call expands into movements (for example Enchufla → Dame → Dile Que No). Shows animated couples in Rueda or Línea Moderna formation; a Step mode is in development. No voice, song upload or beat detection is mentioned. — [ruedasimulator.com](https://ruedasimulator.com/); [GitHub](https://github.com/RNPGhost/RuedaSimulator)
- Salsa Practice's voice coach gives the beats and talks you through combinations. — [salsaforums](https://www.salsaforums.com/threads/i-made-an-app-to-practice-salsa-steps-at-home.42516/)
- Open source: **salsaCountingProgramPython** listens through a mic or plays an MP3, shows the count 1–8, and has a "This beat is the 1" correction button. It shows numbers, not a voice. — [GitHub selvadurai](https://github.com/selvadurai/salsaCountingProgramPython)
- Academic: **SalsaAsst** (Dong, Liu, Chen, Lee; IEEE MASS 2017) is a mobile beat-counting system for salsa that gives voice or vibration prompts. It was tested on 40 salsa songs and the authors claim it beats general trackers. No public app found. — [Stevens research page](https://researchwith.stevens.edu/en/publications/salsaasst-beat-counting-system-empowered-by-mobile-devices-to-ass/)
- No dedicated rueda caller app was found on Google Play or the App Store. Searches turned up only web resources (Rueda.Casino video modules, ruedawiki.org). — [rueda.casino](https://rueda.casino/rueda/); [ruedawiki.org](https://ruedawiki.org/)
- No GitHub project specific to salsa beat or downbeat tracking was found. Only general trackers (BeatNet and others) appeared. — [GitHub downbeat-tracking topic](https://github.com/topics/downbeat-tracking)

### Inferences
- The user's player (feature 6) appears to be new as a combination. Pieces exist separately: counts over real songs (Dancetime), a hand-set 1 (Cadencia, salsaCounting), move calls at a BPM (Ritmo, Rueda Simulator) and clave or count trainers (Salsa Rhythm). **No result showed a count in the user's own recorded voice, or figure calls placed on a song's analysed beat grid.**
- The user's model of ML beats as a suggestion plus a user-tapped 1 matches what the field does. Cadencia and salsaCounting both let the user fix the 1, and the academic work notes that general trackers struggle with salsa.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed whether Ritmo plays real music under its calls; the only source is a third-party listing summary, not the App Store page.
- Dancetime's FAQ and reviews were not read in full for whether it can count your own uploaded files.

## Are there apps for building a personal library of moves, recording your own videos, mapping transitions, or building combos?

### Takeaway
Yes, and this is where the user's app has real, recent competition. **Flow State: Dance Library** (iOS, released October 2026) has separate salsa and bachata libraries, combos cut from your own videos, and a **Move Map: a graph of moves and positions that shows what leads into what, and where the gaps are**. That is very close to features 2 and 3. Several simpler video-library and journal apps exist. None of them checks timing (start count or length) or schedules practice by urgency, except Repertoire and rhythm-review, which add spaced repetition.

### Cited Findings
- **Flow State: Dance Library** (Kevin Kadak, a solo dancer-developer; iPhone only, iOS 17+). Combos come from trimming your own clips frame by frame, marking where each move ends, and labelling steps from your own move list. **Move Map**: "graph of moves (purple) and positions (gold)"; each combo draws its transitions; selecting a move shows what leads into it and what it leads to, with clips; "see which transitions you have and where gaps remain"; one map per style. There are separate Bachata and Salsa libraries. Notes can be pinned to a video timestamp. Live Sessions track time and steps, with Lock Screen and Dynamic Island support. Discover is a social feed where you can adopt others' combos into your Move Map. Freemium: 10 private combos free, Pro US$6.99/month or $69.99/year (CAD $9.99/$99.99). v1.0 is dated "Oct 15"; v1.1.2–1.3.0 shipped within days of capture, so this is a new app (© 2026). No ratings yet. — [feelflowstate.com](https://feelflowstate.com/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/flow-state-dance-library/id6760927859)
- **Repertoire** (myrepertoire.io): turns class recordings and reels into separate moves, gives a page of looping clips for the moves you pick, uses spaced repetition so "moves stay in rotation until mastered", and flags moves that are getting rusty. Claims "most dancers forget 9 of 10 moves". Platform, price and dances were not confirmed (fetch failed). — [myrepertoire.io (search snippet)](https://myrepertoire.io/)
- **Salsa & Bachata: DanceLog** (HunCut / John Michael Escobar Celi; iOS, Mac and Vision). A class and practice journal with date, duration, teacher and notes. Its move library is sorted by style (On1, On2, Cuban) and by status (to learn, practicing, achieved, mastered). It tracks weekly minutes and goals. Premium adds an adjustable-tempo salsa counter, photos and videos, and export: $6.99/month, $34.99/year, $44.99 lifetime. Offline, no account. v1.0 Aug 19, v1.0.7 Oct 21 (2026 implied). No ratings yet. Bachata only appears in session text. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/salsa-bachata-dancelog/id6801615017)
- **Sweet Dance Moves Library** (Buildandteach Media; iOS, iPad, Mac). Saves videos from the camera roll, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok or Facebook, tags them by style and source, builds practice lists, plays local videos in slow motion, and syncs through iCloud. Covers salsa, bachata, kizomba, zouk and tango. $2.99 lifetime or $9.99/year. 5.0★ from 2 ratings; v1.0 on 2025-09-17, v3.5. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sweet-dance-moves-library/id6749212354)
- **DanceLib** (DanceLib SA; iOS). A "MyLib" personal library where you upload your own videos, with tags, looping, speed control and progress tracking, across 11 styles including salsa. Subscription. Latest version seen is 1.0.54 (2025-04-23); no ratings. Activity after that is unknown. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dancelib/id6670781631)
- **La Puerta DS** and **move2move** appeared in searches for move-library apps; details not checked. — [La Puerta DS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6446827032); [move2move](https://apps.apple.com/th/app/move2move/id6503006708)
- **rhythm-review** (GitHub, Waidhoferj): moves with names, descriptions and count durations; patterns made of moves; practice sets; practice logs; **SM-2 spaced repetition**. Built with TypeScript, Svelte, Drizzle and Postgres (a stack strikingly like the user's). 0 stars, 10 commits, demo on Vercel. A hobby project. — [GitHub](https://github.com/Waidhoferj/rhythm-review)
- **Dance.io | Mirror & Loop & Slow** (iOS, any style): A-B loop, speed, mirror, camera overlay and notes on videos. This is the general video-practice tool closest to the user's feature 7. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dance-io-%E9%95%9C%E5%83%8F-%E5%BE%AA%E7%8E%AF-%E5%87%8F%E9%80%9F-%E6%95%B0%E6%8B%8D-%E5%AF%B9%E6%AF%94-%E7%AC%94%E8%AE%B0/id6748628387?l=vi)

### Inferences
- Flow State is the nearest thing to the user's app. It covers the library, own videos, a position/move graph with gaps, salsa and bachata walls, and timestamped notes. It differs in that combos are clips cut from video, not routines checked for timing. It has no start count or length in counts, no music or count player, no drill scheduling, no lessons as a separate object, no saving YouTube links into videos, and no Android. It is social-first.
- **The user's gap report is no longer unique as a concept**: Flow State advertises "where gaps remain". The user's version (positions → what can follow, timing breaks, start-count rules) looks more formal, but this is inferred from marketing copy only.
- Dance-specific spaced repetition exists (Repertoire, rhythm-review, DanceLog's status buckets). The user's frequency-as-priority urgency model is a variant of it, not a first.
- Almost all of these are iOS-only. The user's browser plus Android setup is unusual in the niche.

### Gaps
- Flow State's exact release year was not shown. "Oct 15" plus © 2026 and a 2026-range app id suggest October 2026, but this is unconfirmed.
- Repertoire's platform, price and whether it has combos, transitions or music could not be confirmed.
- No Reddit threads were retrieved, so community adoption and opinion of these apps is unknown.

## Are there practice trackers or schedulers made for dancers?

### Takeaway
A few exist, all small and new: Repertoire (spaced repetition with rusty detection), DanceLog (journal, goals, weekly minutes, mastery status) and rhythm-review (SM-2, a hobby repo). Flow State logs sessions but does not schedule. None found combines scheduling with music-timed practice.

### Cited Findings
- Repertoire: spaced repetition with "moves stay in rotation until mastered" and rusty-move detection. — [myrepertoire.io](https://myrepertoire.io/)
- DanceLog: session logging, calendar, weekly minutes and sessions, goals (1 free, more with Premium), confidence charts, Home Screen widgets. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/salsa-bachata-dancelog/id6801615017)
- rhythm-review: the SM-2 algorithm and practice logs with statistics. — [GitHub](https://github.com/Waidhoferj/rhythm-review)
- Flow State: Sessions, a Journey calendar, step counts. The site does not mention practice scheduling or reminders. — [feelflowstate.com](https://feelflowstate.com/)
- Generic spaced-repetition reminder apps (Never Forget, MemoRep) are used for physical skills too, but are not dance-specific. — [memorep.com](https://memorep.com/en/blog/spaced-repetition-reminder-app); [Never Forget](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/never-forget-spaced-reminder/id1551467159)

### Inferences
- The user's Today screen (urgency from logged sets against a per-exercise frequency, recomputed with nothing stored, with typed exercises and a log popup per type) is closer to a habit or drill scheduler than to recall-based SM-2. The concept exists; the drill-type model (song practice, figure practice, count-only runs logged as sets) appears unmatched.

### Gaps
- Not checked: general dance practice trackers outside Latin dance (ballet, hip-hop), which another researcher may cover.

## Which of the user's features have no equivalent in this space?

### Takeaway
No equivalent found: **the player's figure calls timed to a song's beat grid**, a **count in the user's own recorded voice**, **routines checked for timing breaks by start count and length**, **lesson-video sections linked to figure variations**, and **YouTube save-to-local tied to figures and lessons**. Partial equivalents exist for the library, the transition graph with gaps (Flow State), spaced-repetition scheduling (Repertoire), counts over real songs (Dancetime, Cadencia) and loop, mirror and slow (Dance.io, SMTC). The integration of all of these into one app is not matched by anything found.

### Cited Findings (comparison matrix: closest existing equivalent per feature)
- (1) Today / urgency scheduling: partial in Repertoire (spaced repetition), DanceLog (status, goals) and rhythm-review (SM-2). — [myrepertoire.io](https://myrepertoire.io/); [DanceLog](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/salsa-bachata-dancelog/id6801615017); [rhythm-review](https://github.com/Waidhoferj/rhythm-review)
- (2) Figure library with variations and own recordings: Flow State (move list, combos from own clips), Sweet Dance Moves, DanceLib. **Start count and length in counts:** rhythm-review stores "count durations" per move; nothing else found. — [feelflowstate.com](https://feelflowstate.com/); [rhythm-review](https://github.com/Waidhoferj/rhythm-review)
- (3) Positions graph and gap report: **Flow State Move Map** (moves plus positions, leads-into and leads-to, "where gaps remain"). — [feelflowstate.com](https://feelflowstate.com/)
- (4) Routines checked for timing and connection: Ritmo (routines from moves at a tempo, no checks mentioned), Flow State combos (from video), rhythm-review patterns. No timing or connection validation found. — [Ritmo](https://mwm.ai/apps/ritmo-dance-practice/6778009666)
- (5) Song library with ML beat analysis and a tapped 1: Dancetime (fingerprint catalogue), Cadencia (manual tempo and 1, no detection), Dentro (paid count mapping), salsaCounting (open source, "this beat is the 1"). — [Dancetime](https://dancetimeapp.com/); [Cadencia](https://cadencia.dance/); [Dentro](https://getdentro.com/); [GitHub](https://github.com/selvadurai/salsaCountingProgramPython)
- (6) Player with own-voice count, clave and timed figure calls over a song: the count over a song is in Dancetime; clave and count over generated rhythm in Salsa Rhythm and BeatLab; move calls at a BPM in Ritmo and Rueda Simulator. **The combination, the own recorded voice and calls on a real song's grid: none found.**
- (7) Lesson videos with looped, mirrored and slowed sections linked to figures: loop, slow and mirror exist in Dance.io, SMTC (audio sections) and DanceLib. Flow State pins notes to video timestamps and links moves to combo clips. **Sections linked to figure variations as cards: none exact.** — [Dance.io](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dance-io-%E9%95%9C%E5%83%8F-%E5%BE%AA%E7%8E%AF-%E5%87%8F%E9%80%9F-%E6%95%B0%E6%8B%8D-%E5%AF%B9%E6%AF%94-%E7%AC%94%E8%AE%B0/id6748628387?l=vi); [SMTC](https://showmethecounts.com/)
- (8) Saving YouTube videos and bookmarking sections: Sweet Dance Moves saves links from YouTube, Instagram and TikTok (as links, not downloads). Flow State imports through the share sheet. **A server-side 480p download that becomes a recording or lesson: none found.** — [Sweet Dance Moves](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sweet-dance-moves-library/id6749212354)
- (9) Multi-user and Android: Flow State has a social layer but is iPhone-only. Dancetime, SMTC, Cadencia and BeatLab run on both platforms but have no move library. Most journal apps are iOS-only. — [feelflowstate.com](https://feelflowstate.com/); [Dancetime](https://dancetimeapp.com/)

### Inferences
- The strongest claims to novelty are: (a) **practising your own routine to a real song with your own voice counting and figure names called on the beat**; (b) **timing-aware routines** (start count and length, timing breaks, whether a routine loops); (c) **the tight links** between figure variation, recording, lesson video section, routine and drill schedule.
- The 2025–2026 burst of solo-developer dance-journal apps (Flow State, DanceLog, Sweet Dance Moves, Ritmo, Cadencia, Repertoire) suggests demand, and also that the "personal library plus graph" idea is being reached on its own by others. Flow State in particular came out within weeks of this research.

### Gaps
- Feature comparisons rest mostly on marketing pages and store listings, not hands-on use. Flow State's Move Map and Repertoire's scheduler may be more or less capable than described.
- App Store and Google Play were not searched directly (only through web search), so small apps, especially Android-only ones, may be missing.
